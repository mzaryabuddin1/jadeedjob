Chat Lab

Login two different accounts (A and B) and chat between them.
How to test: sign in as A and B (email or phone + password, or paste a JWT). Then in A use New chat → Direct with B's user id (shown in B's header), send a message and watch it arrive live in B. Try: typing indicator, read ticks, unread badge, mute, block, delete, image upload, and job chat (a job id; the employer side adds the applicant's user id).
